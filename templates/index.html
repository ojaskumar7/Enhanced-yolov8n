Research Overview

Designed for the chaos of Indian streets

Real-time object detection is a critical component of Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS), particularly under complex road conditions. Indian traffic presents a unique challenge: extreme density, diverse vehicle types, unpredictable pedestrian movement, and varied lighting.

Our enhanced YOLOv8n model integrates Convolutional Block Attention Module (CBAM) into the bottleneck and a BiFPN-inspired attention mechanism into the Concat layer — boosting detection accuracy and robustness while maintaining real-time performance suitable for intelligent transportation systems.

The model was trained on the DriveIndia dataset developed by TiHAN, IIT Hyderabad — 12,500 images spanning 24 object categories across 120 hours and 3,400 km of diverse Indian driving.
